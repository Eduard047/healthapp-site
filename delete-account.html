Delete your account and data

App: HealthApp for Android and iPhone · Developer: Eduard Martynenko · Last updated: 9 November 2026

iPhone: there is no account to delete

The iPhone version of HealthApp has no account, no sign-up and no cloud sync. The optional account exists only in the Android app for now. On iPhone nothing leaves your device except the Oura sign-in, which uses Oura's own OAuth, so there is no cloud copy to delete. Your data is removed in one of two ways:

  1. In the app, open Settings with the gear icon at the top of the Today screen, open Data and privacy («Данные и приватность»), tap Delete all my data («Удалить все мои данные») and confirm.
  2. Or delete the app from your iPhone. This removes everything the app stored on the device.

To also remove what other services hold, disconnect Oura in Settings (this deletes the token stored on your phone) and revoke access in your Oura account; manage Apple Health data in the Health app.

Android: delete your account in the app

HealthApp accounts are optional and exist only in the Android app for now. If you created one on Android, you can delete it from the app at any time. The app follows your phone's language (Russian, English or Ukrainian); the English labels are shown first, with the Russian ones in brackets.

  1. Open HealthApp and tap the gear icon at the top of the Today screen to open Settings.
  2. Open Account and cloud («Аккаунт и облако»).
  3. Tap Delete account («Удалить аккаунт»).
  4. Type the confirmation word DELETE («УДАЛИТЬ» in Russian) and confirm with Delete («Удалить»).

The app then asks the server to delete your account. Your login and all your cloud records are removed in the same request, and the app confirms with Account deleted («Аккаунт удалён»).

In Ukrainian the labels are «Обліковий запис і хмара», «Видалити обліковий запис» and the confirmation word «ВИДАЛИТИ».

Or request deletion by email

If you cannot open the app (for example you uninstalled it or lost your phone), email martynenkoeduard1@gmail.com from the address you used to sign in, with the subject line:

HealthApp account deletion

I will delete the account and its cloud data and confirm by reply, no later than 30 days after your request.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address and sign-in sessions. Deleted immediately.
  • The cloud copy of your own records (body measurements, blood pressure, water, food, mood, symptoms, tags, breathing sessions, medications and supplements, manual workouts, goals, gear, notification rules, settings). Deleted immediately, together with the account.
  • Deletion markers (an entry ID and table name, no content). Removed together with the account. For records you delete while keeping your account, a daily job purges the markers after 180 days.

If you only want to remove the cloud records and keep the login, use Delete cloud copy («Удалить копию в облаке») on the same screen.

What is not deleted

  • Data on your phone. Deleting the account keeps the local data. On Android remove it with Delete all my data («Удалить все мои данные») in Settings, Data and privacy («Данные и приватность»), or by uninstalling the app.
  • Data held by Oura, Garmin, Health Connect and Apple Health. These services are separate from HealthApp: manage that data in your Oura account, your Garmin account, Health Connect (Android) and the Health app (iPhone). HealthApp only reads from Health Connect and Apple Health and never writes to them, so deleting your account does not change them. Disconnecting a source in the app deletes the tokens stored on your phone.
  • Files you exported yourself.
  • Backups and delivery logs that Supabase and Resend may keep for a limited time under their own policies.
