Delete your account and data

App: HealthApp for Android · Developer: Eduard Martynenko · Last updated: 31 October 2026

Delete your account in the app

HealthApp accounts are optional. If you created one, you can delete it from the app at any time. The app's interface is in Russian, so the Russian labels are shown with their meaning.

  1. Open HealthApp and tap the gear icon at the top of the Today screen to open Settings.
  2. Open «Аккаунт и облако» (Account and cloud sync).
  3. Tap «Удалить аккаунт» (Delete account).
  4. Type the confirmation word «УДАЛИТЬ» and confirm with «Удалить».

The app then asks the server to delete your account. Your login and all your cloud records are removed in the same request, and the app confirms with «Аккаунт удалён» (Account deleted).

Or request deletion by email

If you cannot open the app (for example you uninstalled it or lost your phone), email martynenkoeduard1@gmail.com from the address you used to sign in, with the subject line:

HealthApp account deletion

I will delete the account and its cloud data and confirm by reply, no later than 30 days after your request.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address and sign-in sessions. Deleted immediately.
  • The cloud copy of your own records (body measurements, blood pressure, water, food, mood, symptoms, tags, breathing sessions, medications and supplements, manual workouts, goals, gear, notification rules, settings). Deleted immediately, together with the account.
  • Deletion markers (an entry ID and table name, no content). Removed together with the account. For records you delete while keeping your account, a daily job purges the markers after 180 days.

If you only want to remove the cloud records and keep the login, use «Удалить копию в облаке» (Delete cloud copy) on the same screen.

What is not deleted

  • Data on your phone. Deleting the account keeps the local data. Remove it with «Удалить все мои данные» (Delete all my data) in Settings, «Данные и приватность» (Data and privacy), or by uninstalling the app.
  • Data held by Oura, Garmin and Health Connect. These services are separate from HealthApp: manage that data in your Oura account, your Garmin account and Health Connect. Disconnecting a source in the app deletes the tokens stored on your phone.
  • Files you exported yourself.
  • Backups and delivery logs that Supabase and Resend may keep for a limited time under their own policies.
