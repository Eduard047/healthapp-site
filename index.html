HealthApp

A personal recovery dashboard for Android.

HealthApp combines data from your Oura Ring and Garmin devices into one view: sleep, readiness, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, training load and recovery. All processing happens on your phone.

What it does

  • Reads your Oura data through the official Oura API after you sign in with your Oura account.
  • Reads data from Health Connect and Garmin when you connect them.
  • Computes daily recovery and health status scores locally.
  • Lets you add your own tags, workouts, weight and notes.

Status

HealthApp is in private development and is not yet published on Google Play.
