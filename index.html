One recovery dashboard for Oura and Garmin.

HealthApp brings sleep, readiness, heart rate variability, resting heart rate and training load from your ring and your watch into one calm, private view. All scores are computed on your phone.

Oura RingSleep, readiness, HRV, SpO2, stress and tags through the official Oura API.
GarminTraining status, load, VO2max, activities and Body Battery.
Health ConnectSteps, heart rate, sleep and workouts from other apps on your phone.
Your own notesTags, manual workouts, weight, goals and symptoms.

Private by design

Your data lives in a local database on your device and access tokens are stored encrypted. Cloud sync is optional: create an account only if you want backup and several devices. No advertising, no analytics. Read the privacy policy.
