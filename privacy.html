Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Summary

HealthApp stores your health data on your own device. If you choose to create an account, the app can additionally back up and sync your data to HealthApp's cloud storage so you can use it on several devices. HealthApp does not sell your data and does not share it with third parties for advertising or analytics.

What data the app processes

  • Oura data – sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate, SpO2, stress, workouts, tags and basic profile information (age, height, weight, biological sex), read through the official Oura API after you sign in with your Oura account.
  • Health Connect data – steps, heart rate, sleep sessions, weight, SpO2 and exercise sessions, read only after you grant the corresponding Health Connect permissions.
  • Garmin data – daily summaries, heart rate, training status and activities, when you connect a Garmin account.
  • Data you enter – tags, notes, manual workouts, weight, goals and profile settings.

Where the data is stored

By default all data is stored in a local database on your phone, protected by the Android app sandbox. Access tokens for Oura and Garmin are stored encrypted on the device and are never uploaded.

Optional account and cloud sync

You can use HealthApp without an account. If you create one, the app syncs your health data and settings to HealthApp's cloud storage, hosted on Supabase in the European Union, so that your data is backed up and available on your other devices. Data is encrypted in transit, stored under your account only, and used solely to provide sync and backup. You can turn sync off or delete your account and all cloud data from the app settings at any time; deletion removes the cloud copy.

How the data is used

The app uses the data only to show it to you and to compute scores such as recovery, health status and training load on the device. Raw responses from the Oura API are not stored; only the values needed for the app's features are kept.

Third parties

  • Oura – the app connects to the Oura API on your behalf. Oura's own privacy policy applies to your Oura account.
  • Google Health Connect – data is read from Health Connect on your device. It is uploaded only if you enable cloud sync with your own account.
  • Garmin – the app signs in to Garmin Connect with your credentials to read your data. Garmin's privacy policy applies to your Garmin account.
  • Supabase – the hosting provider for the optional cloud sync. Supabase processes the data on HealthApp's behalf under its privacy policy; it has no independent use of your data.

The app does not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Sharing and export

You can export your data (PDF reports, GPX tracks, a full data export) from the app. Exported files go only where you send them with the Android share sheet.

Deleting your data

You can disconnect Oura, Health Connect or Garmin at any time in the app settings; this deletes the stored tokens. "Delete all data" in the settings removes the local database and, if you have an account, the cloud copy as well. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stored on the device. To revoke the app's access to your Oura account, use the Oura account settings.

Children

HealthApp is not intended for children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
